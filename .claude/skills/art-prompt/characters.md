# Реєстр зовнішності — Dragon of Icespire Peak

Канонічні описи для генерації арту. Скіл [art-prompt](SKILL.md) вставляє блок `Канон` **дослівно** у промпт — саме це тримає персонажів однаковими на всіх зображеннях.

**Правила ведення:**
- Блок `Канон` — англійською, суцільним текстом, без списків. Тільки те, що незмінне: тіло, обличчя, постійний одяг і спорядження.
- Пози, емоції, дії, ракурси сюди **не пишемо** — це мінливе, воно дописується в промпті окремо.
- Правити опис можна будь-коли. Після правки всі наступні арти йдуть за новою версією.
- Нових персонажів додаємо за шаблоном у кінці файлу.

## 🎭 Персонажі паті

### Луффі (Luffi)

**Аліаси:** Луффі, Луффи, Luffi
**Роль:** Паладин 5, Клятва Відплати · бронзовий драконороджений

**Канон:**
> Luffi, a male bronze dragonborn paladin. A powerfully built draconic humanoid, tall and broad-shouldered, with a thick neck, heavy digitigrade legs and clawed hands. His head is a blunt-snouted draconic muzzle with amber slit-pupilled eyes and a pair of short, thick horns swept back along the skull. His scales are warm burnished bronze, darker along the spine and paler across the throat and chest. His most distinctive feature: scattered across his scales are darker markings shaped like iron crosses, set into the plating like old brands — they run across his shoulders, forearms and the sides of his muzzle. He wears finely woven mithral chain mail with a cool silver-blue sheen over a deep blue padded gambeson, and carries a large Spellguard Shield whose rim is etched with faint arcane runes glowing pale blue. A longsword with a subtle enchanted gleam along the blade hangs at his hip, and a holy symbol rests on his chest. His bearing is disciplined, grave and unhurried.

**Альтернативна форма — кротолюдина** *(прокляття крототропії)*
> A hulking mole-man: a hunched, heavily muscled humanoid mole covered in dense dark-brown velvet fur, with a pink pointed snout, tiny near-blind eyes squeezed shut against the light, and enormous spade-like forepaws ending in long curved digging claws. Traces of his bronze dragonborn build remain in the heavy shoulders and thick tail. His armour hangs torn and half-shed, his weapons abandoned on the ground around him. His posture is feral and hunched, mouth open in a snarl.

### Адольф (Adolf)

**Аліаси:** Адольф, Адольфа, Adolf
**Роль:** Бард 5, Коледж Мечів · тіфлінг

**Канон:**
> Adolf, a male tiefling bard of the College of Swords. Lean and athletic with an acrobat's build, of average height, light and balanced on his feet. His skin is a deep crimson red, his eyes solid dark with no visible whites, and a pair of thick ridged horns curve back and upward from his forehead. He has long dark hair worn loose and a slender pointed tail. He wears well-fitted studded leather armour beneath a finely cut travelling coat with a performer's flourish — good cloth, a touch of deliberate showiness at the collar and cuffs. A rapier with a swept guard hangs at his hip, and an old lute is slung across his back: visibly worn, scuffed and far older than the rest of his gear, an instrument inherited from his grandfather. His expression is confident and faintly mocking.

**Альтернативна форма — кротолюдина** *(прокляття крототропії)*
> A hunched mole-man: a compact, wiry humanoid mole covered in dense dark fur with a reddish cast, a pink pointed snout, tiny near-blind eyes screwed shut, and oversized spade-like forepaws with long digging claws. His horns still curve back from the furred skull. His coat and armour hang torn, his rapier and lute abandoned on the ground. His posture is low, feral and coiled.

### Потужній (Potuzhniy)

**Аліаси:** Потужній, Потужного, Потужнім, Potuzhniy
**Роль:** Чарівник 5, Школа Втілення · людина
**Статус:** ⚠️ Відсутній у паті — Незламній затягнув його в портал

**Канон:**
> Potuzhniy, a male human wizard of the School of Evocation. Slight and unathletic, of modest height, with sharp restless eyes and the perpetually half-distracted look of a man doing arithmetic behind his own conversation. He wears a practical arcane robe rather than a ceremonial one — dark, hard-wearing cloth covered in pockets, loops and buckled straps. A heavy leather-bound spellbook is carried at his side as an arcane focus, a plain quarterstaff in hand, and a pair of Goggles of the Night — dark round lenses in a brass frame — pushed up onto his forehead. A gnarled Staff of Birdcalls is strapped alongside his pack. His clothes and hair are slightly dishevelled, as though he dressed while thinking about something else.

### Незламній (Nezlamniy)

**Аліаси:** Незламній, Незламнія, Незламнію, батько Потужнія, Nezlamniy
**Роль:** Могутній чаклун, батько Потужнія · кредитор паті на 1000 зм з кожного

**Канон:**
> Nezlamniy, an imposing older human archmage and the father of Potuzhniy. Tall and straight-backed, with iron-grey hair swept back from a high forehead, a close-trimmed beard and a hard, unimpressed stare — the family resemblance to his son is obvious in the eyes and the sharp nose, but where the son is dishevelled the father is immaculate. He wears heavy layered robes of deep violet and black, embroidered with silver sigil-work at the hem and cuffs, with rings on several fingers. He carries no visible weapon and needs none; the air around his hands distorts faintly with restrained arcane power, and torn violet portal-light tends to linger at the edges of any scene he has just entered. His manner is that of a man interrupting something beneath his attention.

### Факторія (Faktoria)

**Аліаси:** Факторія, Факторії, Faktoria
**Роль:** Ґномка-винахідниця з Ґноменгарду · супутниця паті

**Канон:**
> Faktoria, a female rock gnome inventor and recluse. Very small and wiry, with a mane of frizzy hair escaping in all directions from under a pair of oversized magnifying goggles strapped to her head. Her face is smudged with soot and grease, her expression permanently mid-thought and slightly manic. She wears a heavy canvas work apron over practical gnomish clothing, hung with tools, spanners, spools of wire and half-finished mechanisms; more tools protrude from every pocket. Her fingers are stained and calloused. She is accompanied by her crossbow platform: a heavy wheeled wooden mounting bristling with four large mounted crossbows, cranks, ratchets and tensioned cabling.

> Cryovain, a young white dragon. A large but not yet ancient wyrm, lean and predatory, with a long sinuous neck and tail and broad membranous wings. His scales are glacial white shading to pale blue-grey along the spine and wing membranes, frost visibly rimed along their edges. His head is narrow and wedge-shaped with a high spined crest sweeping back from the skull, a jagged underbite of icicle-like teeth, and pale ice-blue eyes. Cold vapour trails constantly from his nostrils and jaws, and hoarfrost spreads across whatever surface he stands on.

### Вілдара (Vyldara)

**Аліаси:** Вілдара, Вілдари, Вілдару, банші, банші-ельфійка, привид Аксхолму, Vyldara, banshee
**Роль:** Банші, дух ув'язненої мунельфійки-посланниці · остання перешкода в Аксхолмі

**Канон:**
> Vyldara, the banshee of Axeholm — the vengeful spirit of a moon elf ambassador, now an undead apparition. A luminous, wispy female elven form with no legs, its lower body trailing away into a thin ribbon of drifting spectral mist instead of feet. Her face still carries elven bone structure — high cheekbones, a narrow jaw — but the flesh is translucent and colourless, lit from within by a cold pale light, the eyes sunken hollows of darker glowing radiance. A tangle of long gossamer hair drifts and stirs around her head as though underwater, moving with no wind. Her body is wrapped in tattered spectral rags — the remains of once-fine ambassadorial robes, now reduced to torn translucent streamers that flutter and stream around her like smoke. She casts no shadow and her entire form is faintly self-luminous, pale bluish-white light bleeding outward into the air around her. Her default expression is a mask of cold fury, mouth open mid-scream.

### Бомбур (Bombur)

**Аліаси:** Бомбур, Бомбура, Бомбуру, Bombur, старий дварф
**Роль:** Старий щитовий дварф зі Стоунгіллського заїзду · єдиний живий свідок подій в Аксхолмі

**Канон:**
> Bombur, an old shield dwarf well past a hundred years. Short and heavily built, thick through the chest and shoulders, with the stooped, barrel-bodied frame of someone who has spent a lifetime rolling casks. His long grey beard is plaited into two thick braids weighted with plain iron rings, and his bald crown is fringed with the same grey hair. His face is broad and deeply lined, the nose bulbous and reddened by decades of drink, the eyes small, pouched and tired. He wears a stained leather cellarman's apron over a coarse woollen shirt with the sleeves pushed up thick forearms, heavy boots, and a ring of cellar keys and a tapping mallet hanging at his belt. He is rarely without a dented pewter tankard in one fist. His manner is gruff, weary and closed-off.
